“Your bone structure, features, and coloring combined with your vision are all factors we consider to create a personalized look made exclusively for you.”

With more than 30 years of experience at the forefront of Bergen County's luxury hair care scene, Ayfer Deniz is known for intuitive consultation, architectural cutting technique and multi-dimensional color. As founder and owner of the renowned Ayfer Salon on Closter Dock Road in Closter, NJ, she built a loyal clientele who value Manhattan-caliber artistry in a peaceful, welcoming setting.

An avid student of fashion, fine art and contemporary technique, Ayfer has styled hair for runway productions and fashion editorial shoots, and has been featured in People, Harper's Bazaar and 201 Magazine for her dimensional blondes and rich brunettes.

For 19 years Ayfer welcomed clients at Ayfer Salon in Closter, and later at Salon Pavel in Tenafly. Today, at her own studio on South Summit Street, she welcomes guests to an unhurried space where curated European formulas and personal care take center stage.
